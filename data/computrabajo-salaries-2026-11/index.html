How the figures were read

  • One search per job title and country on that country's Computrabajo site, up to 100 listings whose title contains the job title (a search for vendedor also returns other jobs; those are not counted).
  • Only salaries shown in the listing count. Yearly, two week and weekly pay is converted to a month; daily or hourly pay and salaries without a period are left out.
  • Placeholder figures (like 1,111,111 or 123,456) and values more than 5 times away from the median are left out.
  • A cell needs at least 5 salaries; with fewer it shows "too few".
  • Only public listing pages were read, with robots.txt obeyed. No contact data and no names of people are kept.

Get the same data with Python

from apify_client import ApifyClient
import pandas as pd

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("v0iddo/computrabajo-scraper").call(run_input={
    "keywords": ["contador"],
    "countries": ["mx", "co", "pe"],
    "salaryOnly": True,
    "maxItems": 300,
})
df = pd.DataFrame(client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items())
monthly = df[df["salaryInterval"] == "month"]
# median monthly pay per country, November 2026
print(monthly.groupby(["countryCode", "salaryCurrency"])["salaryMin"].median())
